Summary

  • You can use the app without an account. In that case all your data stays on your phone alone; nothing reaches us.
  • Your health-related data (weight, body measurements, date of birth, sex, training and nutrition records) reaches our server in two situations only, and only if you explicitly consent: when you press "Back up to cloud", and when you link with a trainer.
  • Report photos: if your trainer puts a photo question in a report and you have turned on photo permission, the body photos you pick from your gallery go to your trainer only; they stay on the server for at most 7 days and on the trainer's phone for at most 24 hours after first being opened (section 3.4.1).
  • No ads, no tracking, no data sales. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising library.
  • Our server runs in Germany (Frankfurt). Google's infrastructure is used for notifications and "Sign in with Google"; on iPhone, Apple's infrastructure is also used for "Sign in with Apple", notification delivery and App Store payments.
  • You can have your account and all of your server-side data deleted at any time (section 11).

3. What we process, and where

3.1 Data that stays on your phone (for everyone)

The core of the App works offline. The following is kept in the app database on your phone and does not leave the device unless you ask it to:

  • Training records: programs, cycles, templates, the sets you perform (reps, weight), completed workouts.
  • Nutrition records: meals, recipes, nutrition plans and cycles, "eaten / skipped" marks.
  • Body measurements: weight, circumference measurements and any other measurements you enter, with their dates.
  • Profile: height, sex, date of birth, name, username, e-mail, unit and notification preferences.
  • Progress photos: photos you add from your gallery under My photos are kept in the app's own folder; they are not included in the cloud backup, nor in Android's or iPhone's (iCloud) device backup.

We cannot see this data. Android's automatic backup (Google Drive) and phone-to-phone transfer are disabled for this app; the only route to our server is the button described in 3.3. On iPhone, if your phone's iCloud backup is turned on, this data may also be included in the device backup that Apple keeps; Apple stores that backup and we cannot see it. When you uninstall the App, this data is deleted.

3.2 Account data

An account is optional; it is required for cloud backup and trainer features.

  • Sign in with Google or with Apple: an account can only be opened with your Google account or (on iPhone) your Apple account. Only your account's e-mail address and Google user ID are taken from Google. Only your e-mail address - or, if you chose to hide it, the relay address Apple creates for you - and your Apple user ID are taken from Apple. We do not take your name or your profile picture. No password is created in the App; your password stays with Google or Apple and never reaches us.
  • Technical access records: information such as sign-in time and IP address is kept in our infrastructure provider's authentication system for security purposes.

Where it is kept: Supabase (section 6). Duration: until your account is deleted.

3.3 Cloud backup (only if you ask for it)

When you press "Back up to cloud" on the Profile screen, all the records listed in 3.1 (training, nutrition, measurement and profile) are copied to our server under your account. You trigger the first backup yourself and your explicit consent is requested at that moment (section 4); after you consent, the App refreshes your backup by itself once a week when it starts. With "Restore from cloud" you can download the same data to another phone.

Because this backup contains health-related data, your explicit consent is requested before the first backup (section 4). The backup also keeps a "deleted" trace of records you removed, so that deletions carry over correctly between two devices. This trace is kept for at most 7 days and is then permanently deleted from the server.

A record of the consent you gave at the first backup (the type of consent and its date) is also held on the server. The "Delete cloud backup" button on the Profile screen deletes your backup and this consent record together.

Duration: until you delete the backup or delete your account.

3.4 Trainer link (only if you enter a code)

When you enter the single-use code given to you by a trainer, a link is created with that trainer. While the link lasts, the following are held on our server and shown on your trainer's screen:

  • The name you type when entering the code. It is shown to that trainer only; it is deleted when the link ends.
  • The training programs and nutrition plans your trainer assigns to you.
  • Only if you turn them on (each with its own switch, off by default):
    • Adherence data: how many assigned workouts you did, how many assigned meals you ticked - as counts; sent with your report answer.
    • Body measurements: the latest value, date and change from the previous value of each measurement you have entered since the link began.
    • Muscle distribution: the percentage share of muscle groups, based on the workouts your trainer assigned and you completed.
    • Workout summaries: the date, duration, total volume, exercise and set summary of the workouts your trainer assigned and you completed.
    • Photos requested in reports: up to four body photos (front, back, right, left) that you pick from your gallery and send when your trainer puts a photo question in a report and - if your trainer uses an iPhone and takes a screenshot of these photos - the time it was taken. Details in 3.4.1.
  • The report questions your trainer prepares and the answers you give (free text or choice). What you write in your answers is up to you; you are not obliged to share health information.
  • If you are 15-17 years old, the "I have my parent's consent" statement you tick.
  • The link itself: which trainer, for which service (training / nutrition), start and end dates, reason for ending.
  • So that your trainer can manage the link: your Premium end date (the link cannot run past it), whether the assigned program reached your phone, and on which day you answered each report.
Your trainer sees you under the name you type when entering the code; that name is shown to no other user. Your trainer cannot see your e-mail address, the workouts and meals you set up yourself, your measurements from before the link, the items you keep off, or your cloud backup. Whose student you are is not shown to other users.

Duration: when the link ends (because you leave, the trainer removes you, or the term expires) the assignments, reports, report settings and the name you typed are deleted by the following day. When you turn off sharing of measurements, muscle distribution or workout summaries, that item's copy on the server is deleted at once; when you turn off adherence sharing, no new count is sent, and the counts in report answers you already sent stay with those answers. The link record itself (identifiers, dates, reason for ending) is kept until your account is deleted, to support re-linking and to prevent abuse.

3.4.1 Report photos (only if you allow it)

Your trainer may add a photo question to the weekly report. You receive this question only if you have turned on the "Photos requested in reports" switch on the Trainers page; the switch is off by default. While it is off, your trainer's photo question is never sent to you.

  • How they are sent: you pick the photos yourself from your phone's gallery, for each report (up to four: front, back, right, left). The App does not open the camera and does not ask for access to your whole gallery; only the photos you pick are read. Each photo is reduced in size on your phone before it is sent.
  • Where they are kept: in a closed file store with no public address that only you and your trainer can reach (Supabase, Frankfurt / Germany).
  • How long they stay: the photos are deleted from the server as soon as your trainer's phone downloads them; if they are not downloaded, they are deleted from the server at the latest 7 days after your answer. Your trainer can see the photos for at most 24 hours from the moment they first open them in the App; after that they are deleted from the trainer's phone as well. Photos that are never opened are deleted from the trainer's phone 7 days after your answer. If the link ends, the photos are deleted together with the report.
  • On the trainer's phone: the photos are kept in the app's own folder; they are not saved to the phone's gallery, not included in the device backup, and the App has no save or share button. On Android, screenshots and screen recording are blocked while the photos are open. On iPhone, the photos are hidden while screen recording or screen mirroring is on. Because iPhone does not allow screenshots to be blocked, if your trainer takes a screenshot while the photos are open, you receive a notification and this shows with its date on the report you answered (3.7).
  • Limits: no app can prevent someone from photographing the screen with another device. Under the Trainer Agreement your trainer may not move these photos outside the App, keep them or share them.
  • Withdrawing: you can turn the switch off at any time; once it is off, no new photo question reaches you. Photos you sent before are deleted on the schedule above.

Send only photos of yourself; do not send photos of anyone else.

3.5 Trainer profile (for those who become trainers)

When you become a trainer you create a commercial profile visible to other users: your real name, profile picture, WhatsApp line, biography, the services you offer, your "I am over 18" statement, and the date and version of the agreement you accepted. You choose who can see your WhatsApp number (everyone / my students only / no one). The profile picture is kept at a publicly accessible file address; anyone who knows the link can view it.

Duration: until you stop being a trainer or delete your account.

3.6 Ratings and reviews

You may give a trainer whose student you are a rating from 1 to 5 and a review of up to 500 characters; the trainer may reply to your review. Reviews are published to other users inside the App without showing your name. You can delete your rating and review at any time.

3.7 Notifications

For notifications about new assignments, reports, answers and expiring terms, and for the notification telling you that your trainer took a screenshot of your report photos (3.4.1), your phone's Firebase notification token and your app language are held on the server. The notification text (for example your trainer's name and "assigned a new program") is delivered from our server to your phone through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging service; on iPhone this delivery also passes through Apple's notification service (APNs). The token is deleted when you sign out. You can turn notifications off in the app settings. Records of notifications sent are deleted within 30 days at the latest. The time a screenshot was taken is also written to the report record and deleted together with the report.

3.8 Blocking

When you block a user, that record is held on the server and that person's reviews are not shown to you. When you unblock them, the record is deleted.

3.9 Reporting

When you report a review or a trainer profile, your report (the target, the reason and your explanation if you gave one) is held on the server for review. Only the data controller sees this record; your identity is not disclosed to the person reported. When your account is deleted, your report records are deleted with it.

3.10 Payment

Subscriptions, and the one-time packages that trainers buy for their students, are purchased through Google Play on Android and through the App Store on iPhone. Your card and payment details stay with Google or Apple; they never reach us. Only the verification record of the purchase (product, plan, status, expiry date and the store's transaction number) reaches us; we verify it within Google's or Apple's system and store it linked to your account. The coaching fee you pay your trainer is entirely outside the App; we neither see nor process it.

When a trainer buys a package, a package code is created on the server: the code itself, the length of the package and the trainer who bought it. When you use that code, the account that used it and the start and end dates of your entitlement are recorded as well. When you delete your account, the link between the record and your account is removed; the record itself is kept until the account of the trainer who bought the package is deleted.

Cancelling a subscription and requesting a refund are handled through the store you bought from (Google Play or the App Store) under that store's refund policy. The statutory withdrawal and refund rights of users in the European Union are not affected.

3.11 What we do not collect

We do not collect location, contacts, microphone or an advertising ID. The camera is not used. No general access to your gallery is requested: the App reads only the photos you pick (for a trainer profile picture, progress photos and report photos). The App contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising library. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. No automated decision-making or profiling is carried out about you; training and nutrition programs are produced from your own choices, inside your phone.

3.12 Closed test period

During the closed test before the App's public release, everyone with an account can use Premium and the trainer account free of charge until the test period ends. Store review accounts and accounts opened with Sign in with Apple (the iPhone version is not released during this period) are excluded. No new data is processed for this: only your account's creation date (3.2) is checked. When the test period ends, free use ends automatically; if you have not paid, your trainer links close the same way as for anyone whose Premium or trainer account has ended (3.4).

4. Health data and explicit consent

Your weight, body measurements, date of birth, sex, and your training and nutrition records may count as special categories of personal data (health data) under KVKK. This data leaves your phone through two gates only, and at both of them your explicit consent is requested first:

  1. Cloud backup - the first time you press "Back up to cloud".
  2. Trainer link - while you are entering the trainer code. Adherence data, body measurements, muscle distribution, workout summaries and report photos are not part of this consent: for each of them you give explicit consent separately by turning on that item's switch on the Trainers page.

If you do not consent, only that feature stops working; the rest of the App is unaffected. To withdraw your consent you can delete your cloud backup with the "Delete cloud backup" button on the Profile screen (section 11), turn off an item you share with your trainer (including photo permission) on the Trainers page, and end the trainer link. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out until then.

Whether you write health information into the report answers you give your trainer is your decision. The App is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

7. Transfers abroad

Our servers are in Germany; Supabase, Google and Apple are US companies. When you open an account, your data is transferred to these countries. Under KVKK art. 9 this transfer rests on your explicit consent: you approve it when you open an account and at the cloud-backup and trainer-link gates. If you use the App without an account, no data goes abroad - or anywhere else at all.

7.1 Users outside Turkey

This policy applies to all users, whichever country you are in; we grant the rights in section 10 to everyone. KVKK is the law of Turkey, where the data controller is established. If you live in the European Union / European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR) also applies:

  • Legal bases are the same as in the table in section 5: performance of the contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)), legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)), legal obligation (art. 6(1)(c)); and explicit consent for health data (art. 9(2)(a)).
  • Additional rights: to receive your data in a machine-readable format (data portability), to request restriction of processing, to withdraw your consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your own country.
  • Transfers: your data is held within the EU (Germany). Transfers to Google, to Apple and to Supabase's US entity are covered by those companies' standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, their EU-US Data Privacy Framework certifications.

Any additional rights granted to you by local law in other countries are reserved; you may use the same address to apply.

7.2 United States - Washington State residents

If you live in Washington State, the My Health My Data Act applies to you as well. That law treats information such as your weight, body measurements, nutrition and physical activity as consumer health data. The following is what the law asks us to state plainly; all of it is also described elsewhere in this policy.

  • Consumer health data we collect: weight and body measurements, height, date of birth, gender, workout records, nutrition records and - if you choose to write them - your answers to your coach's report questions and the body photos you choose to send (section 3).
  • Where it comes from: only from what you enter in the App. We do not buy or gather data from any other source.
  • Why we collect it: to build your training program and nutrition plan, to keep your cloud backup and - if you have linked to one - to run your coach's service. It is never used for advertising, profiling or research.
  • Who we share it with: only the coach you choose (for as long as the link lasts) and the service providers that process data on our behalf - Supabase (database, file storage), Google (notifications, sign-in, Play) and, on iPhone, Apple (sign-in, notification delivery, App Store). The full list is in section 6.
  • No sale: we do not sell your consumer health data and will not. The law requires a separate signed authorization for any sale; we never ask for one.
  • No geofencing: the App collects no location data, and we place no virtual boundary (geofence) around health care facilities.
  • Your rights: to know what consumer health data we hold and who it has been shared with, to have it deleted, and to withdraw a consent you have given. You can have it deleted from inside the App (Profile → Delete my account) or by e-mail; you withdraw consent by deleting your cloud backup, turning off an item you share with your coach and ending your coach link (section 4 and section 11).

Requests go to the same address as section 10: in1destek@gmail.com. We answer within 45 days; if we need longer we may extend once by a further 45 days and will tell you. If we refuse a request we explain why and how to appeal. You also have the right to complain to the Washington State Attorney General.

9. Security

All communication between the phone and the server is encrypted (TLS). On the server every record is tied to its owner: thanks to row-level access rules, a user can reach only their own records, the link data opened to them, and publicly available content (trainer profiles, ratings and reviews). The App holds no passwords; sign-in is through your Google or Apple account. Report photos are kept in a separate store with no public address and access rules. Server administration access belongs to the data controller alone. No system is one hundred per cent secure; if a data breach occurs we will notify the Personal Data Protection Board and the affected users within the period required by KVKK.

10. Your rights (KVKK art. 11)

In relation to your personal data you may request:

  • To learn whether it is processed and, if so, to request information about it,
  • To learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used in line with that purpose,
  • To know the third parties to whom it is transferred, in Turkey or abroad,
  • To have it corrected if it is incomplete or inaccurate,
  • To have it erased or destroyed,
  • To have correction and erasure notified to the parties it was transferred to,
  • To object to an outcome against you produced solely by automated analysis,
  • To claim compensation if you suffer damage because of unlawful processing.
How to apply: write from your account's e-mail address to in1destek@gmail.com, with "KVKK request" in the subject line. Your application is answered free of charge within 30 days at the latest. Writing from your account's e-mail address is enough to verify your identity (if you can no longer access that address, see 11.1). If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (kvkk.gov.tr).

What you can do yourself inside the App: correct your profile information, delete measurements and records, delete your review, delete your cloud backup, unblock users, end the trainer link, turn off photo permission, delete progress photos, sign out, turn off notifications.

11. Deleting your account and data

You can have your account deleted in two ways:

  • Inside the app: Profile → Delete my account.
  • By e-mail: write "Delete my account" from your account's e-mail address to in1destek@gmail.com.

What is deleted: your account, your cloud backup, your link records, the assignments and reports belonging to you (including report photos), your ratings and reviews, your blocking and report records, your consent records, your trainer profile and picture if any, and your notification tokens. All of it is permanently deleted within 30 days at the latest. If you are a trainer, your active students' links are closed; content already assigned to them stays on their own phones until its planned end.

The records on your phone are not affected by account deletion; you delete those by uninstalling the app. If you want only your cloud backup deleted without closing your account, use Profile → "Delete cloud backup" or write to the same address.

11.1 If you can no longer access your Google or Apple account

Your account in the App is the Google or Apple account you signed in with; the App has no separate password and no account recovery of its own. If you lose access to that account, you get it back through Google's or Apple's account recovery steps; we are not able to do this for you.

  • We do not move data to another account. Your cloud backup, your trainer link and your package code are not transferred to a new account you open: we cannot verify that two accounts belong to the same person, and giving your data to the wrong person would be a greater harm than losing it. The records on your phone stay where they are; you can make a new cloud backup with your new account. Your subscription is tied to your account in the store you bought from (Google Play or the App Store); you restore it through that store itself.
  • Deleting an account you can no longer access: if you cannot write from the account's e-mail address, write from another address to in1destek@gmail.com, giving the account's e-mail address and information that can show the account is yours (for example roughly when you opened it, or the name of the trainer you were linked to). If this information lets us reasonably verify that the account is yours, we delete it; if it does not, we tell you so, with the reason, within 30 days at the latest. This check is needed to protect against requests trying to delete someone else's account.

12. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. To create a trainer link you must be at least 15 years old; for ages 15-17 a statement of parental consent is required. The statement is not verified; responsibility lies with the person making it and their parent or guardian. To become a trainer you must be over 18. If you believe we are processing a child's data without consent, write to us and we will delete the record.
