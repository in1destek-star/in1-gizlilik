Summary

  • You can use the app without an account. In that case all your data stays on your phone alone; nothing reaches us.
  • Your health-related data (weight, body measurements, date of birth, sex, training and nutrition records) reaches our server in two situations only, and only if you explicitly consent: when you press "Back up to cloud", and when you link with a trainer.
  • No ads, no tracking, no data sales. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising library.
  • Our server runs in Germany (Frankfurt). Google's infrastructure is used for notifications and "Sign in with Google".
  • You can have your account and all of your server-side data deleted at any time (section 11).

2. Scope

This text covers the Android version of the App. The privacy policies of third parties such as Google Play, your Google account and our infrastructure provider Supabase apply separately; you will find links in section 6.

3. What we process, and where

3.1 Data that stays on your phone (for everyone)

The core of the App works offline. The following is kept in the app database on your phone and does not leave the device unless you ask it to:

  • Training records: programs, cycles, templates, the sets you perform (reps, weight), completed workouts.
  • Nutrition records: meals, recipes, nutrition plans and cycles, "eaten / skipped" marks.
  • Body measurements: weight, circumference measurements and any other measurements you enter, with their dates.
  • Profile: height, sex, date of birth, name, username, e-mail, unit and notification preferences.

We cannot see this data. Android's automatic backup (Google Drive) and phone-to-phone transfer are disabled for this app; the only route to the cloud is the button described in 3.3. When you uninstall the App, this data is deleted.

3.2 Account data

An account is optional; it is required for cloud backup and trainer features.

  • Sign-up by e-mail: your e-mail address and password. The password is stored irreversibly (as a hash); we cannot see it. Verification and password-reset messages are sent to that address.
  • Sign in with Google: only your account's e-mail address and Google user ID are taken from Google. We do not take your name or your Google profile picture.
  • Technical access records: information such as sign-in time and IP address is kept in our infrastructure provider's authentication system for security purposes.

Where it is kept: Supabase (section 6). Duration: until your account is deleted.

3.3 Cloud backup (only if you ask for it)

When you press "Back up to cloud" on the Profile screen, all the records listed in 3.1 (training, nutrition, measurement and profile) are copied to our server under your account. You trigger the first backup yourself and your explicit consent is requested at that moment (section 4); after you consent, the App refreshes your backup by itself once a week when it starts. With "Restore from cloud" you can download the same data to another phone.

Because this backup contains health-related data, your explicit consent is requested before the first backup (section 4). The backup also keeps a "deleted" trace of records you removed, so that deletions carry over correctly between two devices.

Duration: until you have the backup deleted or delete your account.

3.4 Trainer link (only if you enter a code)

When you enter the single-use code given to you by a trainer, a link is created with that trainer. While the link lasts, the following are held on our server and shown on your trainer's screen:

  • The name you type when entering the code. It is shown to that trainer only; it is deleted when the link ends.
  • The training programs and nutrition plans your trainer assigns to you.
  • Adherence data: whether you did the assigned workout, whether you followed the assigned nutrition plan — as counts; not set or meal detail.
  • The report questions your trainer prepares and the answers you give (free text or choice). What you write in your answers is up to you; you are not obliged to share health information.
  • If you are 15-17 years old, the "I have my parent's consent" statement you tick.
  • The link itself: which trainer, for which service (training / nutrition), start and end dates, reason for ending.
Your trainer sees you under the name you type when entering the code; that name is shown to no other user. Your trainer cannot see your e-mail address, the records you keep on your own, your measurements or your cloud backup. Whose student you are is not shown to other users.

Duration: when the link ends (because you leave, the trainer removes you, or the term expires) the assignments, reports, report settings and the name you typed are deleted by the following day. The link record itself (identifiers, dates, reason for ending) is kept until your account is deleted, to support re-linking and to prevent abuse.

3.5 Trainer profile (for those who become trainers)

When you become a trainer you create a commercial profile visible to other users: your real name, profile picture, WhatsApp line, biography, the services you offer, your "I am over 18" statement, and the date and version of the agreement you accepted. You choose who can see your WhatsApp number (everyone / my students only / no one). The profile picture is kept at a publicly accessible file address; anyone who knows the link can view it.

Duration: until you stop being a trainer or delete your account.

3.6 Ratings and reviews

You may give a trainer whose student you are a rating from 1 to 5 and a review of up to 500 characters; the trainer may reply to your review. Reviews are published to other users inside the App without showing your name. You can delete your rating and review at any time.

3.7 Notifications

For notifications about new assignments, reports, answers and expiring terms, your phone's Firebase notification token and your app language are held on the server. The notification text (for example your trainer's name and "assigned a new program") is delivered from our server to your phone through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging service. The token is deleted when you sign out. You can turn notifications off in the app settings. Records of notifications sent are deleted within 30 days at the latest.

3.8 Blocking

When you block a user, that record is held on the server and that person's reviews are not shown to you. When you unblock them, the record is deleted.

3.9 Payment

Subscriptions are purchased through Google Play. Your card and payment details stay with Google; they never reach us. Only whether the subscription is valid reaches us, and we verify that within Google's system. The coaching fee you pay your trainer is entirely outside the App; we neither see nor process it.

3.10 What we do not collect

We do not collect location, contacts, camera, microphone, your files or an advertising ID. The App contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising library. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. No automated decision-making or profiling is carried out about you; training and nutrition programs are produced from your own choices, inside your phone.

4. Health data and explicit consent

Your weight, body measurements, date of birth, sex, and your training and nutrition records may count as special categories of personal data (health data) under KVKK. This data leaves your phone through two gates only, and at both of them your explicit consent is requested first:

  1. Cloud backup — the first time you press "Back up to cloud".
  2. Trainer link — while you are entering the trainer code.

If you do not consent, only that feature stops working; the rest of the App is unaffected. To withdraw your consent you can have your cloud backup deleted (section 11) and end the trainer link. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out until then.

Whether you write health information into the report answers you give your trainer is your decision. The App is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

7. Transfers abroad

Our servers are in Germany; Supabase and Google are US companies. When you open an account, your data is transferred to these countries. Under KVKK art. 9 this transfer rests on your explicit consent: you approve it when you open an account and at the cloud-backup and trainer-link gates. If you use the App without an account, no data goes abroad — or anywhere else at all.

7.1 Users outside Turkey

This policy applies to all users, whichever country you are in; we grant the rights in section 10 to everyone. KVKK is the law of Turkey, where the data controller is established. If you live in the European Union / European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR) also applies:

  • Legal bases are the same as in the table in section 5: performance of the contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)), legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)), legal obligation (art. 6(1)(c)); and explicit consent for health data (art. 9(2)(a)).
  • Additional rights: to receive your data in a machine-readable format (data portability), to request restriction of processing, to withdraw your consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your own country.
  • Transfers: your data is held within the EU (Germany). Transfers to Google and to Supabase's US entity are covered by those companies' standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, their EU-US Data Privacy Framework certifications.

Any additional rights granted to you by local law in other countries are reserved; you may use the same address to apply.

9. Security

All communication between the phone and the server is encrypted (TLS). On the server every record is tied to its owner: thanks to row-level access rules, a user can reach only their own records, the link data opened to them, and publicly available content (trainer profiles, ratings and reviews). Passwords are stored as hashes. Server administration access belongs to the data controller alone. No system is one hundred per cent secure; if a data breach occurs we will notify the Personal Data Protection Board and the affected users within the period required by KVKK.

10. Your rights (KVKK art. 11)

In relation to your personal data you may request:

  • To learn whether it is processed and, if so, to request information about it,
  • To learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used in line with that purpose,
  • To know the third parties to whom it is transferred, in Turkey or abroad,
  • To have it corrected if it is incomplete or inaccurate,
  • To have it erased or destroyed,
  • To have correction and erasure notified to the parties it was transferred to,
  • To object to an outcome against you produced solely by automated analysis,
  • To claim compensation if you suffer damage because of unlawful processing.
How to apply: write from your account's e-mail address to in1destek@gmail.com, with "KVKK request" in the subject line. Your application is answered free of charge within 30 days at the latest. Writing from your account's e-mail address is enough to verify your identity. If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (kvkk.gov.tr).

What you can do yourself inside the App: correct your profile information, delete measurements and records, delete your review, end the trainer link, sign out, turn off notifications.

11. Deleting your account and data

You can have your account deleted in two ways:

  • Inside the app: Profile → Delete my account.
  • By e-mail: write "Delete my account" from your account's e-mail address to in1destek@gmail.com.

What is deleted: your account, your cloud backup, your link records, the assignments and reports belonging to you, your ratings and reviews, your trainer profile and picture if any, and your notification tokens. All of it is permanently deleted within 30 days at the latest. If you are a trainer, your active students' links are closed; content already assigned to them stays on their own phones until its planned end.

The records on your phone are not affected by account deletion; you delete those by uninstalling the app. If you want only your cloud backup deleted without closing your account, writing to the same address is enough.

12. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. To create a trainer link you must be at least 15 years old; for ages 15-17 a statement of parental consent is required. The statement is not verified; responsibility lies with the person making it and their parent or guardian. To become a trainer you must be over 18. If you believe we are processing a child's data without consent, write to us and we will delete the record.
